# Thinking

Getting aligned before building: interviews, shared domain language, and
pulling answers out of the people who hold them.

## User-invoked

Run only when you type them.

- **[grill-me](./grill-me/SKILL.md)**: Sharpen a plan or design through relentless interview.
- **[grill-with-docs](./grill-with-docs/SKILL.md)**: Interview that also maintains the glossary and ADRs.
- **[to-questionnaire](./to-questionnaire/SKILL.md)**: Write a questionnaire for the person who can answer a decision.
- **[wait-what](./wait-what/SKILL.md)**: Re-explain the last message in plain English with context.

## Model-invoked

You can type them, and the agent also reaches for them when a task fits.

- **[domain-modeling](./domain-modeling/SKILL.md)**: Sharpen domain terms and record them in GLOSSARY.md and ADRs.
- **[grilling](./grilling/SKILL.md)**: Stress-test a plan one round of questions at a time.
- **[research](./research/SKILL.md)**: Research a question against primary sources, with citations.
