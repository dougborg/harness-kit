# Banning em dashes

harness-kit's house style does not ban em dashes in skills or docs.

## Why this is out of scope

The house style aims for short, plain prose. A ban on one punctuation mark
doesn't make prose shorter or plainer, and enforcing it would churn every
file for no change in what an agent does.
